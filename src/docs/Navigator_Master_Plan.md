# 🧭 Navigator Master Plan

## Vision

Navigator is an interactive coaching application designed to guide retention specialists through real customer conversations.

The goal is not to provide scripts.

The goal is to guide critical thinking, improve active listening, and present the right coaching tools at the right time.

---

# Core Philosophy

Every conversation follows the same rhythm.

Ask

↓

STOP

↓

Listen

↓

Diagnose

↓

Choose ONE coaching tool

↓

STOP

↓

Listen

↓

Continue

The application should never overwhelm the representative with every possible rebuttal.

Instead, it should reveal coaching tools as they become appropriate.

---

# Roadmap Structure

Every roadmap follows the same stages.

1. Discovery
2. Diagnosis
3. Education
4. Commitment
5. Resolution

Conditional stages may appear.

• Attempt to Save

• Final Attempt

---

# Discovery Stage

Purpose

Understand the customer's true concern before recommending solutions.

Sections

• Stage Goal

• Opening Question

• STOP & Listen

• Probing Questions

• STOP & Listen

• Listen For

• Coach Notes

---

# Diagnosis Stage

Purpose

Identify the customer's situation.

Available Coaching Tools

• Empathy

• Tie Downs

• Analogies

• Rebuttals

• Situation-Based Coaching

---

# Commitment Stage

Help the customer agree to the next step.

---

# Resolution Stage

Confirm next steps.

Respect the customer's final decision.

---

# Conditional Coaching

If the customer declines:

Attempt to Save

↓

If customer still declines:

Final Attempt

↓

If customer still declines:

Proceed with Cancellation

# Design Principles

One question at a time.

Stop talking.

Listen.

Never educate before diagnosing.

Choose one coaching tool.

Do not overwhelm the representative.

Guide the conversation naturally.
